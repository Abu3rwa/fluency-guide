## 2. Eligibility

To book private sessions, you must:
- Be at least 18 years old, or have parental/guardian consent if under 18.
- Provide accurate contact information, including a valid WhatsApp phone number for communication (common in Libya and supported regions).
- Not be prohibited from using our Services by applicable law.

Instructors must be verified Sudanglish educators with active profiles.

## 3. Booking Process

### 3.1 Session Selection
- Browse available session types created by instructors via the Student Booking Interface.
- Each session type specifies duration (e.g., 30-60 minutes), price (in LYD or USD), and description.
- Select an instructor, date (future dates only), and available time slot.

### 3.2 Confirmation
- Upon submission, your booking enters "pending" status.
- You will receive confirmation via WhatsApp within 24 hours.
- Sessions are confirmed only after instructor approval and payment.

### 3.3 Guest Bookings
- Non-registered users can book as guests but must provide name, email, and phone.
- Guests are encouraged to create accounts for better tracking and history.

## 4. Payment Terms

### 4.1 Pricing

### 4.2 Payment Methods

- Refunds are subject to cancellation policy (see Section 5).

### 4.3 Taxes and Fees

## 5. Cancellation and Refund Policy

### 5.1 Student Cancellations
- **24+ hours before session:** Full refund (minus 5% processing fee).
- **12-24 hours before:** 50% refund.
- **Less than 12 hours:** No refund; session marked as no-show.
- Cancellations via booking interface or WhatsApp.

### 5.2 Instructor Cancellations
- Full refund to student if instructor cancels.
- Instructor may reschedule with student consent.
- Repeated cancellations may result in instructor suspension.

### 5.3 No-Shows
- Students: Forfeit payment; session not rescheduled.
- Instructors: Full refund + compensation to student (next session 50% off).

### 5.4 Force Majeure
- No refunds for cancellations due to unforeseen events (e.g., internet outages, natural disasters). Rescheduling offered where possible.
